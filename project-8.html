Overview

This project is an aimed to defend against small group 1/2 drone swarms. It relies on a modular ground station that uses a mix of radar and computer vision to accurately identify threats while filtering out clutter like birds or bad weather. Once a target is locked, the system dispatches a group of autonomous quadcopters that work together to kinetically intercept and neutralize the drones. This system offers a highly scalable defense that a small fraction of the cost of legacy military alternatives, and is easily integrable with existing platforms.
