Yellow Jacket Swarm Counter Unmanned Aerial System

Machining GNC Product Design Computer Vision Computer Aided Design (CAD)

Overview

I am currently building an affordable, autonomous counter-drone (C-UAS) defense system designed to function as a rapidly deployable Iron Dome against smaller Group 1 and 2 aerial threats. Operating under my startup, Interstellar Foundry, this Create-X capstone project centers on a low-cost, modular ground station that uses edge computing to fuse microwave radar and computer vision data. The sensor handshake accurately detects and tracks evasive targets and coordinates a swarm of quick response quadcopter interceptors to kinetically defeat the threat. By utilizing commercial off-the-shelf components and rapid manufacturing techniques, I am developing a scalable, tactical perimeter defense solution that dramatically undercuts the cost and complexity of legacy systems.
