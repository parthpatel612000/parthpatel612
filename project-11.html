Intelligence Assessments: Chinese Air Superiority and Quantum Security

Intelligence Analysis Analytical Writing War Gaming Strategic Intelligence

Overview

These papers feature two comprehensive intelligence estimates that analyze the approach China is taking to secure future air superiority. The first report examines the rapid development of the Chinese advanced stealth fighter fleet and long range strike networks that threaten regional stability. The second analysis explores the disruptive impact of quantum technology on the intelligence community and highlights how Chinese quantum advancements could completely neutralize the stealth advantages of rival fifth generation aircraft like the F35 and B21. Together these projects illustrate a dual strategy where China actively expands both its own stealth combat capabilities and its quantum sensors to render adversary stealth platforms obsolete.
